## Context

I kept starting productivity systems and abandoning them after three weeks. The problem wasn't motivation — it was that each system was designed by someone else, for someone else's life.

I spent a month building one from scratch, treating it like a product with a real user: me.

## What I Built

A Notion workspace with three layers: a daily review (10 min, morning + evening), a weekly planning session (45 min, Sunday), and a decision log where I record any choice that took me more than five minutes to make and why.

The decision log is the most valuable piece. Reviewing it quarterly is the closest thing I've found to accelerated experience.

## What I Learned

- Systems break at the seams between tools — the simpler the stack, the longer it survives
- A daily review only works if it's short enough to feel effortless on your worst day
- Writing down decisions forces clarity that thinking alone never does
