## Context

I was doing a heavy outreach push — investors, potential co-founders, operators I wanted to learn from. I needed to track state (sent, replied, follow-up due) without spending money on a CRM I'd use for six weeks.

## What I Built

A Google Sheets setup with an Apps Script backend that: pulls reply status from Gmail via the API, auto-labels threads, calculates days-since-contact, and sends me a daily digest of who needs a follow-up.

## What I Learned

- Google Apps Script has surprisingly good Gmail API access with almost no setup
- The bottleneck in outreach is never the tooling — it's the quality of the message
- Tracking response rates by subject line variant taught me more about copywriting than any course did
