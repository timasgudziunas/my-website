## Context

I wanted to learn what it actually felt like to charge strangers money for software. Not a course, not a tutorial — just build something, put a price on it, and see what happens.

I gave myself 90 days and a single goal: $500 MRR.

## What I Built

A tool that automatically generates SEO-optimized FAQ sections from a URL. You paste a link, it scrapes the page, generates 10–15 questions real users ask, and outputs clean HTML you can drop into your site.

Launched on Product Hunt (got 80 upvotes), posted on Reddit, cold-emailed 40 agencies.

## What Happened

Ended at $120 MRR across 6 paying customers. Paused it rather than shut it down — the customers who paid loved it, but I couldn't figure out the acquisition channel fast enough to justify continuing.

## What I Learned

- $120 MRR is not a failure. It's proof that strangers will pay you for something you built in a weekend
- Product Hunt traffic doesn't convert — it validates, at best
- The unit economics worked; the growth channel didn't. Those are different problems
